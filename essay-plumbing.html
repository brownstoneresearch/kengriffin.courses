PLUMBING

The trade is not the position

A print is an agreement. A position is what remains after clearing and settlement have done their work. Between those two facts sit margin calls, collateral, settlement cycles, and the possibility that the other name does not appear.

Variation margin turns price change into cash today. Initial margin is the buffer the clearer demands for tomorrow. A fail is a settlement that did not happen on time. Corporate actions rewrite the claim under your feet. Holiday calendars decide whether “T+1” was ever a day you had.

Most public stories of blow-ups are told as narratives about genius or folly. The operational record is usually shorter: collateral was late, a calendar was missed, a clearer asked for cash that had been assumed to be optional.

Drill. Trace one instrument from order to cash: venue, clearer, settlement date, margin rule. Draw it as six boxes. The box you cannot fill is the one that can surprise you.
