Edge is a statement about the other side

Edge is not a feeling that a price will rise. Edge is a specific claim: someone in this room is acting on a clock, a constraint, or a model that you can describe — and that description would be wrong if X were true.

Forced sellers, rebalancing, option hedging, inventory dump by a dealer, a rule that forbids a holder from waiting — these are other-side stories. “It looks cheap” is not. Cheap against what? Who is willing to sell it to you, and why don’t they want it?

If you cannot name the other side, you do not have edge. You have a seat in the room and a preference. Those are allowed. They should not be renamed.

Drill. Write one sentence that starts “The other side is…” and one that starts “This would be false if…”. If either sentence could fit any name, start over.
