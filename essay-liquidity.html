LIQUIDITY

A quote is not an exit

A bid you cannot hit in size is a suggestion. Liquidity is the right to transfer risk without becoming the event.

Four measures, always together:

  • Tightness — the spread at the inside.
  • Depth — size posted near that inside.
  • Immediacy — how quickly size appears when you need it.
  • Resilience — how the book refills after it is taken.

Capacity is the practical limit: how much you can do before your own order is the news. Impact is the bill for ignoring capacity. Quiet names with beautiful stories often fail this test first.

Drill. For one name, estimate how much you could do in a day without becoming the tape. Write the number. Then write what you would do if that number were a tenth of what you hoped.
