METHOD

A week is not finished until the writing is.

Each week has a briefing, a drill, and a review. Vague language is returned.

1 · Briefing

A short recorded note. One idea. No montage.

2 · Drill

A map, a checklist, or a memo. Length is capped so the thought has to finish.

3 · Review

Comments on clarity, evidence, and risk language — not on whether a view is fashionable.

The memo bar

  • What is the question?
  • What would change your mind?
  • What can you lose?
  • What is the evidence, with sources?
  • What is the decision, in one paragraph?

If a sentence cannot survive those five questions, it does not ship.
