RISK

Name the loss first

A thesis that leads with upside is a brochure. The market does not owe you the brochure.

Write four lines before the story:

  • Market — what move in the underlying, over what horizon, does damage.
  • Liquidity — what extra you lose because you must trade to get out.
  • Credit — whose name sits on the other side, and what failure looks like.
  • Operations — clocks, wires, corporate actions, human error.

Leverage multiplies all four. It is not a fifth idea. If you cannot put numbers or named events on those lines, you are not managing risk. You are hoping the room stays polite.

Drill. Take any position you can describe. Fill the four lines in units you can feel. If a line is “it should be fine,” delete the position from the page until the line is honest.
