THE ACADEMY

Standards before scale.

We exist to teach the unglamorous work that compounds: reading, risk, and the ability to put a decision on paper.

Why this name

A documented public path — a student, a satellite dish, a standard that did not loosen with size — is useful as a case. It is studied the way a writing school studies a novelist: for craft, not for access.

It is not his classroom. It is not an invitation into a firm.
