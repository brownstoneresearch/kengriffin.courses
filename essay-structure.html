STRUCTURE

The room is the product

People speak of “the market” as if it were a single weather. It is a set of rooms. Each room has a door policy, a speaking rule, and a way of writing down what happened.

A displayed limit book is a public argument. A dealer market is a series of conversations. A crossing network is a quiet match. An RFQ is a request that may receive no answer. The same issuer can exist in all four rooms on the same morning and print four different stories.

Before you discuss a name, write the room: venue, matching rule, what is published, who is allowed to speak. If that paragraph is vague, the rest of the analysis is decoration on a market you have not identified.

Drill. Pick one instrument you think you know. Write six lines: venue, hours, matching rule, disclosure, clearer, settlement. The blanks are the curriculum.
