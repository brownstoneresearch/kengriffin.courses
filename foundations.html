How the weeks run

Each week: a briefing of under forty minutes, a reading that is short on purpose, a drill with a hard length cap, and a review that marks clarity — not whether the reviewer “likes” a view.

  • About five hours of work
  • Office hours once a week
  • Twenty-five seats
  • No live trading inside the course

What completion means

You can describe a market without metaphors. You can name the other side. You can say what you can lose. You can put a decision on one page. You cannot claim a track record from this term, and we will not invent one for you.

Education only. The market remains yours to enter or to leave.
