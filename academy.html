ABOUT

An academy of standards.

We teach the unglamorous work that compounds: reading, risk, and the ability to put a decision on paper.

Why this name

Kenneth Cordele Griffin’s public story is useful because it is specific. A student. A satellite dish. A standard that did not loosen as the firm grew. That arc is studied here as a case in intensity and craft — the same way a writing school might study a novelist.

It is not an invitation into his firm. It is not his classroom. It is not a claim of access.

Independence. Ken Cordele Griffin Academy is an independent project on kengriffin.courses. It is not affiliated with Citadel LLC, Citadel Securities, or Kenneth C. Griffin. No course promises employment, introductions, or investment returns.
