MANDATE

If it is not the market, it is out of scope.

In

  • Venues, participants, and incentives
  • Instruments: cash, futures, options, credit, FX
  • Price formation and the limit order book
  • Liquidity, impact, and capacity
  • Risk: market, liquidity, counterparty, operational
  • Information, noise, and the research memo
  • Clearing, settlement, margin, and collateral

Out

  • Stock picks and “signals”
  • Lifestyle, branding, or motivation as the product
  • Promises of employment at any firm
  • Claims of inside information
  • Guaranteed returns

The aim is competence in the market — not a story about the market.

FIVE DOMAINS

The whole subject, held in one map.

01 Structure

Who may trade, on what venue, under what rule. Exchanges, dealers, dark pools, RFQ. Why the same name prints two different prices.

02 Liquidity

The ability to transfer risk without moving the world. Depth, tightness, immediacy, resilience. Capacity is a market fact, not a wish.

03 Risk

What can be lost before the thesis is discussed. Market, liquidity, credit, operational. Units a person can feel.

04 Information

What is priced, what is rumor, what would change your mind. The memo is how information becomes a decision.

05 Plumbing

Clearinghouses, settlement cycles, margin, collateral, fails. Most blow-ups start here, not in the narrative.

Then writing

None of the five domains count until they survive one page another adult can mark.
