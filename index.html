INDEPENDENT · NOT AFFILIATED

Process over prediction

Markets reward repeatable work. You will learn how to frame a question, gather evidence, and write a conclusion someone else can audit.

Risk before return

Return is the residue of risk taken well. The first skill is naming what you can lose, in dollars and in time.

Writing as thinking

If you cannot put the idea on one page, you do not have the idea. Memos are the core drill of the academy.

WHO IT IS FOR

Built for seriousness, not spectators.

  • Beginners who want a standard, not a hot take
  • Career switchers aiming at research, markets, or fintech
  • Operators who need a cleaner model of capital and risk

WHO IT IS NOT FOR

We will not sell you a shortcut.

  • Anyone seeking stock picks or “signals”
  • Anyone expecting a job at Citadel or an introduction
  • Anyone who wants guaranteed returns
